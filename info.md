# Heating Power Card

A compact Home Assistant Lovelace card that shows accumulated heating power from multiple sources on a single animated gauge.

- 🔥 Configurable active/inactive icons and colours
- 📊 Animated gauge bar — solid, gradient, or stacked per-source
- 🔢 Active-source badge (auto-hides when nothing is on)
- ⚫ Per-source indicator dots — gradient, growth, or steady mode
- ⚖️ Automatic unit handling: W, kW, MW (plus per-sensor factor)
- ↔️ Designed for Sections view — 12 columns × 1 row
